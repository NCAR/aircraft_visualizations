Welcome to the RAF flight data visualizer.

Welcome to the RAF Flight Data Visualizer! Dive into 20 years of flight data from the NSF/NCAR Research Aviation Facility (RAF). Explore detailed flight paths, compare data with camera imagery, and discover past projects.

Real-time data from the most recent flight is available on the Real-Time page.

  • Data Selection: Select the project to see the available variables.
  • Filters: Filter data by project, category, flight or search for variables of interest in the variable list.
  • Visualization Type: Depending on the data type, choose between line charts, histograms, and map overlays.

Data used here can be found on the EOL Field Data Archive. Follow the link in the Data Access column of the Past Projects block to access the Low Rate NetCDF file archive for each project to download the data.

Have a question about the RAF Flight Data Visualizer? Email rafsehelp@ucar.edu for comments and suggestions. Find more information about our projects on the EOL Homepage
